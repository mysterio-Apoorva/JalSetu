JAL-SETU pilot release — 11 October 2026

Scope
Real Cognito accounts, AWS persistence, OpenStreetMap tiles, and Open-Meteo recent
daily weather. A resident selects a map point and reports water-outage days,
infants under one year and elders aged 60+. These are self-reported household
counts, not census estimates. The pilot does not promise tanker dispatch.

Priority version pilot-priority-v1
35 * min(daysWithoutWater / 14, 1)
20 * min(consecutive daily rainfall < 1 mm / 30, 1)
15 * clamp((7-day mean temperature Celsius - 25) / 15, 0, 1)
15 * min(infants / 5, 1)
15 * min(elders / 5, 1)
The sum is rounded to 0–100. These are unvalidated pilot policy weights. Each
score includes its weather-through date; resubmit to refresh the report/weather.
No missing weather is converted to zero. Reports leave the board after seven
days; one current report per account avoids double-counting repeated submissions.
Locations are rounded to roughly 100 m and count components are withheld from
the shared board. Reports are household points, not verified ward-wide rankings.

AI simulation
Nova Canvas model amazon.nova-canvas-v1:0, one 512x512 image per simulation.
Asynchronous DynamoDB Stream worker; server-side ownership check; private S3.
Generated scene is wrapped in an SVG carrying a permanent simulation banner.
It never enters AUDIT, REQUEST, delivery volumes, OTP verification or real proof.
One simulation/account/UTC day and a shared daily attempt allowance. Retrying
returns the same job, avoiding duplicate generation. No model-level retry.
Failed/interrupted jobs need operator investigation and never count as delivery.
SimulationDailyLimit defaults to 0 until the operator supplies a spending limit.

User-only steps
Create and email-verify your resident account on the deployed site. Supply the
verified email if it should receive a staff role. Never share your password,
verification code, AWS root login or access keys in chat.
Supply the desired AWS spending allowance and operational alert email. Confirm
the SNS subscription email after its destination is configured.
To enable AI, configure SimulationDailyLimit within that allowance, deploy it,
then run a real image-generation smoke test before calling that path verified.

Remaining operational boundaries
No actual delivery partners, verified collection sites or opening tanker
inventory have been supplied. Physical dispatch remains gated accordingly.
This is a public pilot, not a certified emergency allocation system. Operator
vetting, weather-refresh policy, abuse/load limits, data retention, commercial
weather licensing and the AI path's real invocation test need review before a
larger operational rollout. Existing operations dashboards keep their actual
data/empty states; generated delivery images cannot make missing partners real.
