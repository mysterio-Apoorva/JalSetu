JAL-SETU: real planning data, simulated delivery

Road route
Destination search and road geometry use Amazon Location with HERE data.
The server uses backend/shared/jal-kendras.json; the browser supplies a destination
and optional allowlisted centre ID. Vasant Kunj uses the supplied Plus Code;
Ujwa, Nangloi and Ashok Vihar remain disabled pending exact map pins.
JAL_KENDRA_NAME/LAT/LNG can override the registry with a single operator origin.
Unknown or pending centres are rejected. No invented depot or straight line replaces a road route. Distance and
estimated duration are provider results for truck travel. This is a recommended
road route, not a proof of globally optimal fleet allocation or vehicle-specific
clearance. Physical tanker movement and delivery remain simulated.

Weather
Open-Meteo: https://open-meteo.com/en/docs
30 complete daily observations from the provider's model output, through
yesterday in Asia/Kolkata; seven-day mean temperature and consecutive days with
less than 1 mm precipitation. Source/date are displayed. Missing weather is not
zero-filled. Free service is for non-commercial use; see provider terms.

Population
WorldPop Global 2, 2025, 100 m population raster, CC BY 4.0:
https://api.worldpop.org/v2/
https://api.worldpop.org/v2/openapi.json
Each point selects a fixed 0.02-degree geographic cell, drawn on the map.
The provider calculates population, area and people/km2 for that cell. These
are modeled estimates for 2025, not current census counts or household surveys.
Provider jobs are asynchronous and cached by cell/year in DynamoDB. No API keys
are needed at the standard API limit; see provider documentation for quotas.
Historical reference table: Delhi DES Statistical Abstract 2022, Table 12.8,
Census 2011 district population and density:
https://des.delhi.gov.in/sites/default/files/DES/generic_multiple_files/sabstract2022.pdf
Historical district boundaries differ from current boundaries. These values
are shown as a comparison table, never silently applied to a user's location.

Priority policy version 2
Water outage: 30 points at 14 days; dry spell: 15 at 30 days;
temperature: 10 from 25 to 40 C; infants: 15 at 5 people;
elders aged 60+: 20 at 5 people; density: 10 at 30,000 people/km2.
Each contribution is capped; the sum is rounded to 0-100. Household ages/outage
are resident-reported. Weather and density are loaded server-side. A new score
requires both sources, so missing population/weather cannot be fabricated.
This is a transparent allocation policy, not a clinically validated or trained
prediction model. Older version-1 reports did not include density. Stored
reports show their input date and expire from the board after seven days.

Real email OTP for simulated delivery
Signup verification continues to use Cognito. Trip-confirmation email uses SES
and the signed-in account's verified email, never an arbitrary browser address.
Only salted-by-trip SHA-256 hashes are stored. Codes expire after 10 minutes,
have five attempts, require a 60-second resend interval and are single-use.
A send failure invalidates the code. No raw OTP is returned by the API.
Successful confirmation updates SIM_DELIVERY only, adding zero actual litres.
It never creates an AUDIT or verifies a physical delivery.

Activation inputs
Set OTP_FROM_EMAIL in root .env and verify the SES sender you control.
The configured sender's SES verification status is checked by /planning/config;
reload the page after clicking the AWS verification link to enable sending.
The checked-in centre registry supplies the Vasant Kunj origin. SES sandbox
requires verified recipients too; unrestricted recipient delivery requires AWS
production access: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/ses/latest/dg/request-production-access.html
No sender identity is invented and no production-access request is automatic.
Run scripts/infrastructure.py, prepare_production_backend.py and the reviewed
finish_pilot_release.py --execute workflow, then publish the frontend normally.
The release script preserves existing settings unless a nonempty override is
provided. An empty sender leaves the OTP button disabled with a clear message.

Verification boundaries
Regression tests mock provider responses. Actual provider probes and deployed
Lambda checks are recorded separately under ignored .runtime files. Without a
verified sender/recipient, inbox delivery cannot be claimed as tested.
